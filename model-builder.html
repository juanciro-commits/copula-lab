The copula: each pair turned into percentiles (u, v). It shows only the dependence.

Formulas: joint f(x, y) = c(F(x), G(y)) · f(x) · g(y); conditional f(y | x) = c(F(x), G(y)) · g(y). Copula parameters come from Kendall's τ; families are compared by AIC = 2k − 2·log-likelihood (lower is better). Simulations use a fixed seed, so results repeat. Companion to the Copula Lab.
